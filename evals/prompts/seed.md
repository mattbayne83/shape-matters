# Cycle 5 Seeds — Full Enrichment

Focus areas: Stress-test DCI/Autonomy, new company data, cross-domain analogies.

## From Cycle 4

1. [HIGH] **DCI variance as the decorrelation driver**: Sweep all-same DCI (0 to 100) → measure Spearman correlation between fidelity and authority-agility. Find the DCI distribution that minimizes Spearman. Does our archetype assignment maximize decorrelation?

2. [HIGH] **DCI empirical calibration**: Validate archetype DCI estimates (Valve=95, Nucor=82, Google=58, Meta=28, Haier=88, Amazon=40) against Glassdoor autonomy data, academic studies on hierarchical vs flat decision styles, or published org culture research. Are our numbers defensible?

3. [MED] **Two-pizza blended model**: Amazon's effective org is a mixture: ~70% of decisions at L=2 (team-internal, d=0.5d) and ~30% at full L=9 (cross-cutting, d=3.0d). Compute blended lag health. Does the blended model better describe Amazon's reality?

## New Seeds (Cycle 5)

4. [HIGH] **New reference companies**: Research 2-3 companies to add to the dataset. Candidates: Spotify (squad model, ~8K employees), Bridgewater (~1.5K, radical transparency), W.L. Gore (~12K, lattice org), Netflix (~13K, high-freedom culture), or Toyota (~375K, Gemba Walk origin). For each: estimate levels, headcount, DCI, decision cycle. Run through all models and check if predictions match known organizational behavior.

5. [HIGH] **Cross-domain analogies**: Does the fidelity/lag/autonomy model map to other relay-chain systems? Explore: (a) supply chain bullwhip effect (Forrester 1961), (b) military C2 (OODA loop, Mission Command doctrine), (c) neural network depth vs gradient flow. For each: identify the equivalent of fidelity, lag, and autonomy. Do the same structural limits apply?

6. [MED] **Depth discount validation**: The autonomy formula uses log(3)/log(L) as the depth discount. Is there empirical or theoretical justification for this specific functional form? Could it be log(2)/log(L) or 1/log(L)? Test sensitivity: sweep discount exponents and see which best separates the 6 reference companies into meaningful tiers.
